# Bundled hymns

This is a verified starter set of public domain hymns for the United States: every text was published in 1930 or earlier, so as of 1 Jan 2026 it is in the U.S. public domain. Later verses, translations, and arrangements are omitted unless they are themselves verified. Songs with other licenses — larry-holder, WorshipCommons, CC, NC — are excluded. We did not copy from Faithlife Hymnbase or from music.proclaim.logos.com.
